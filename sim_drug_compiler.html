1. Gasteiger–Marsili (1980) Iterative Orbital Electronegativity (PEOE)

Each atom's orbital electronegativity \(\chi_i\) is expanded as a quadratic function of partial charge \(q_i\) using published hybridization-specific parameters \((a_i, b_i, c_i)\) across \(\text{sp}^3, \text{sp}^2, \text{sp}\) orbitals and implicit hydrogens (\(D_H^+ = 20.02\text{ eV}\)):

6-Iteration Damped Charge Transfer
$$\chi_i^{(k)} = a_i + b_i q_i^{(k-1)} + c_i \big(q_i^{(k-1)}\big)^2, \qquad \Delta q_{i \to j}^{(k)} = \left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^k \frac{\chi_j^{(k)} - \chi_i^{(k)}}{D_{\min(\chi)}^+}, \quad k = 1,\dots,6$$
