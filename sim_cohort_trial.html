1 · Multiplicative Spine Dynamics & Itô-Corrected Log Drift (Art. 07 §10.4)
$$dx_i = \left[\frac{b_{0,i} + d_i(t) + \frac{v_i A_i^{n_i}}{K_i^{n_i} + A_i^{n_i}} - k_{A,i}A_i}{A_i} - D_i\right]dt + \sqrt{2D_i}\,dW_i, \quad x_i = \ln A_i$$
Multiplicative spine fluctuations $\sigma_m A_i\,dW_i$ force logarithmic coordinates where $-D_i = -\sigma_{m,i}^2/2$ shifts true stochastic fixed points $(A_{\text{lo},i}, A_{\text{mid},i}, A_{\text{hi},i})$ relative to deterministic zeros.
2 · Static Heterogeneity $s_i$ vs. Dynamic Fluctuation ($3.3\times$ Inflation Trap)
$$\rho_{\text{auto}}(t) \xrightarrow{t\to\infty} \varphi = \frac{\mathrm{Var}(s)}{\mathrm{Var}(s)+\mathrm{Var}(d)} \approx 0.70, \qquad D = (1-\varphi)\,\mathrm{Var}(x_{\text{obs}})\,k$$
Permanent between-person offset $s_i$ accounts for $\varphi \approx 70\%$ of cross-sectional variance. Naive cross-sectional estimators omit $(1-\varphi)$, inflating $D$ by $1/(1-0.70) \approx 3.33\times$ and understating escape times by orders of magnitude.
3 · Persistence Power Law, $R_i$ vs. $\rho_i$, & Perturbation Separatrix Assay
$$\tau_i \propto \rho_i^{\,\alpha/D_i} \;\;(\alpha/D \approx 23.3), \quad \rho_i = \frac{A_{\text{hi},i}}{A_{\text{mid},i}} \;(R^2 \approx 0.85), \quad R_i = \frac{A_{\text{hi},i}}{A_{\text{lo},i}} \;(R^2 \approx 0.32)$$
During abstinence $d_i \equiv 0 \implies E_d = \partial\ln\tau/\partial\ln d \equiv 0$, whereas $E_v = +21.8$. A transient fractional knockdown $A_i \to (1-p)A_{\text{hi},i}$ flips patient $i$ permanently iff $(1-p)A_{\text{hi},i} < A_{\text{mid},i} \iff \rho_i < \frac{1}{1-p}$.
4 · Pharmacogenomic CYP2D6 Exposure, NMDA Paradoxical Calm & Cardiac QTc
$$C_{p,i} = \frac{\text{Dose}}{f_{\text{2D6}}M_{\text{CYP},i} + (1-f_{\text{2D6}})M_{\text{CES1},i}}, \qquad \text{NMDA}_i = \frac{\gamma_{\text{NMDA},i}}{1 + (\text{DA}_i / K_q)^2}$$
In True NMDA Release-Deficit ADHD (low $\eta_{\text{rel},i}$), pure NDRIs fail to raise $\text{DA}_i$ enough to silence $\gamma_{\text{NMDA},i}$ while raising NE agitation; DAT releasers restore $\text{DA}_i$ and collapse glutamate noise. `CYP2D6 PM` ($0.15\times$) $\times$ subclinical `hERG` reserve yields `1-in-200` $\text{QTc} > 500\text{ ms}$ outliers.
