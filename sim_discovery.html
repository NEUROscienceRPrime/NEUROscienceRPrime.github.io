1. Density-Fitted Kohn–Sham DFT (PySCF Cloud Solver)

    
$$E_{\text{KS}}[\rho] = T_s[\rho] + E_{\text{ext}}[\rho] + E_J^{\text{RI}}[\rho] + E_{\text{xc}}^{\omega\text{B97X-D}}[\rho] + \Delta G_{\text{ddCOSMO}}(\varepsilon = 78.4)$$

    
Range-separated hybrid functional ωB97X-D with dispersion correction and def2-SVP basis + def2-universal-jkfit density fitting inside domain-decomposed COSMO aqueous continuum.

  


  

    
2. Conceptual DFT: Parr Electrophilicity & Condensed Fukui f⁺

    
$$\mu \approx \frac{\varepsilon_{\text{HOMO}} + \varepsilon_{\text{LUMO}}}{2}, \quad \eta \approx \varepsilon_{\text{LUMO}} - \varepsilon_{\text{HOMO}}, \quad \omega = \frac{\mu^2}{2\eta}$$

    
$$f_A^+ = q_A(N) - q_A(N+1)$$

    
Global electrophilicity \(\omega\) ranks warhead aggressiveness (Maleimide \(21.6\text{ eV}\) vs. mild Phenacyl \(7.4\text{ eV}\)); condensed \(f_A^+\) pinpoints the specific electrophilic atom attacked by a Cysteine thiolate.

  


  

    
3. Covalent Thiol Adduct Reaction Energy (CH₃SH Probe)

    
$$\Delta E_{\text{cov}} = E(\text{Ligand–SCH}_3) + E(\text{LG}) - E(\text{Ligand}) - E(\text{CH}_3\text{SH})$$

    
[?] Error Bar: \(\Delta E_{\text{cov}}\) quantifies reaction thermodynamics, not transition-state kinetics \(k_{\text{inact}}/K_I\). Mild phenacyls target \(-8\text{ to }-12\text{ kcal/mol}\) to avoid proteome-wide GSH depletion (\(<-30\text{ kcal/mol}\)).

  


  

    
4. 7-Axis Hendlich (1997) LIGSITE Cavity Volume

    
$$\text{PSP}(\mathbf{r}) = \sum_{d=1}^{7} \mathbb{I}\!\left[\text{protein before } \mathbf{r} \land \text{protein after } \mathbf{r} \text{ along } \hat{\mathbf{u}}_d\right] \ge 6$$

    
$$V_{\text{pocket}} = N_{\text{6-conn}} \cdot h^3, \quad \text{Anti-Hallucination Gate: } V_{\max} < 180\text{ \AA}^3 \implies \text{Refuse Blind Vina}$$

    
Evaluated live in-browser across 3 Cartesian + 4 cubic diagonal axes at \(h = 1.0\text{–}1.2\text{ \AA}\) adaptive spacing with 6-connected BFS clustering.

  


  

    
5. Shrake–Rupley 42-Point Fibonacci Sphere SASA

    
$$\text{SASA}_i = 4\pi (r_{\text{vdW},i} + r_{\text{H}_2\text{O}})^2 \cdot \frac{1}{42}\sum_{k=1}^{42} \mathbb{I}\!\left[\min_{j \neq i} \|\mathbf{p}_{i,k} - \mathbf{x}_j\| > r_{\text{vdW},j} + r_{\text{H}_2\text{O}}\right]$$

    
Used for every Cysteine \(S_\gamma\) (\(r_{\text{vdW}} = 1.80\text{ \AA}, r_{\text{H}_2\text{O}} = 1.40\text{ \AA}\)), paired with an \(8\text{ \AA}\) cationic (LYS/ARG) vs. acidic (ASP/GLU) shell scan and \(<2.5\text{ \AA}\) disulfide detector.
