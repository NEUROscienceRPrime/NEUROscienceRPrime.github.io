Within 8 Å of C172 Sγ sit 6 cationic residues (`K171, R173, R175, R176, JunD R286, K289`) and 0 acids, yet no H-bond donor is `< 4 Å` (nearest polar N: `R173` at **3.85 Å**; nearest cation: **4.29 Å**; SASA **47.6 Å²** apo → **32.2 Å²** DNA-bound).
