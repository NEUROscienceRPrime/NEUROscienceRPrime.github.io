$$\frac{dC_{u,\text{brain},i}}{dt} = k_{\text{BBB},i}\!\left(K_{p,\text{uu},i}\,C_{u,\text{plasma},i} - C_{u,\text{brain},i}\right)$$ $$K_{p,\text{uu}} = \frac{C_{u,\text{brain,ss}}}{C_{u,\text{plasma,ss}}} \propto \exp\!\big(\alpha \log P - \beta\,\text{TPSA}\big) - \Phi_{\text{P-gp}}$$

Article 08 Mechanism: Ethanol inhibits hepatic Carboxylesterase-1 hydrolysis of cocaine to inactive benzoylecgonine (raising parent plasma cocaine) while transesterifying the methyl ester into Cocaethylene (\(t_{1/2} \approx 3.8\text{ hr}\)), which retains DAT affinity, loses the serotonergic SERT brake, and blocks cardiac hERG channels \(\sim 3.2\times\) harder.

Organ 02 // Cheng–Prusoff Brainstem MOR & Sympathetic Adrenergic Split
$$\theta_{\text{MOR,Fent}}(t) = \frac{[F]_b / K_{d,F}}{1 + [F]_b / K_{d,F} + [N]_b / K_{d,N}}, \qquad R_{\text{drive}}(\%) = 100\!\left(1 - \frac{0.94\,\theta_{\text{Fent}}^{2.2}}{0.36^{2.2} + \theta_{\text{Fent}}^{2.2}}\right)$$

Because intranasal naloxone clears with \(t_{1/2} \approx 72\text{ min}\) while lipophilic fentanyl redistributes slowly (\(t_{1/2} \approx 4.2\text{ hr}\)), \(\theta_{\text{MOR,Fent}}(t)\) rebounds past 90 minutes (renarcotization).

Organ 03 // 1D Coupled Transmural Ventricular Wedge (\(N = 18\)) & Emergent Maxwell Dipole ECG
$$C_m \frac{\partial V_k}{\partial t} = g_{k-\frac{1}{2}}\big(V_{k-1} - V_k\big) + g_{k+\frac{1}{2}}\big(V_{k+1} - V_k\big) - I_{\text{ion},k}(V_k, \mathbf{w}_k), \qquad k \in \{1,\dots,18\}$$ $$\Phi_{\text{vent}}(t) = \alpha \sum_{k=1}^{17} \frac{V_k(t) - V_{k+1}(t)}{r_k^2}, \qquad \text{TDR} = \text{APD}_{90,\text{M}} - \text{APD}_{90,\text{Epi}}$$ $$I_{\text{Kr},k} = G_{\text{Kr},k}\big(1 - B_{\text{hERG}}\big)\,x_{r,k}\,r_\infty(V_k)(V_k - E_K), \qquad B_{\text{hERG}} = \frac{[\text{Coc}]/3.8\,\mu\text{M} + [\text{CE}]/1.2\,\mu\text{M}}{1 + [\text{Coc}]/3.8\,\mu\text{M} + [\text{CE}]/1.2\,\mu\text{M}}$$

Emergent Concordant T-Waves & R-on-T Reentry (Gima & Rudy 2002 / Antzelevitch Wedge): Cells \(1\text{–}6\) (Endo), \(7\text{–}12\) (Mid-myocardial M-cells with \(\sim 45\text{–}60\%\) lower \(I_{\text{Ks}}\)), and \(13\text{–}18\) (Epi, high \(I_{\text{to}}\) notch & rapid repolarization) are coupled by gap junctions at \(\Delta t = 0.05\text{ ms}\). Upright concordant T-waves emerge purely because Epicardium repolarizes before M-cells/Endocardium (\(V_{\text{Endo/M}} - V_{\text{Epi}} > 0\)); Cocaine/Cocaethylene \(\text{Na}_V1.5\) blockade physically slows wavefront transit widening QRS; and M-cell Phase-2/3 EADs electrotonically re-excite recovered Epicardium to trigger genuine transmural R-on-T Torsades de Pointes.
